How I score

Every bottle gets a score out of 10, to one decimal place. The numbers mean the same thing for a whiskey as for a fragrance.

9.5 to 10 Exceptional
One of the best I have had. I would replace it the day it ran out.
8.5 to 9.4 Excellent
Does almost everything right. Easy to recommend.
7.5 to 8.4 Very good
Glad to have it. One or two things hold it back.
6.5 to 7.4 Good
Enjoyable, with clear reservations. Right for some people, not for everyone.
5 to 6.4 Fine
Nothing wrong, nothing memorable. Not worth seeking out.
Below 5 Not recommended
Flawed in a way that is hard to get past.

What goes into it

  • Whiskey is scored on nose, palate and finish. Fragrance is scored on scent, longevity, sillage and the bottle itself.
  • Each of those scores comes with a line saying why it earned it.
  • The overall score is my judgment of the whole bottle. It is not an average, so it can sit a little above or below the others.
  • Price never changes a score. A review says in words whether the bottle is worth the money, and shows the price, so you can weigh that for yourself.
  • Bottles and samples. Some reviews come from my own bottle and some from a sample. Each review says which. A fragrance tried from a sample gets no score for the bottle itself.
  • Scores are one person's palate and nose. Use them as a starting point, not a verdict.
